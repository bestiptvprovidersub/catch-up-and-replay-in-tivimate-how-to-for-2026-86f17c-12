Catch-up and replay in TiviMate: how-to for 2026

✍️ Eleanor Parker · 📅 October 2026 · ⏱️ 4 min read · 🛡️ Verified Review

Catch-up lets you watch shows that aired up to 72 hours ago. Replay restarts the currently playing program from the beginning.

This guide shows you exactly how to use both features in TiviMate 5.x with a premium IPTV subscription like SubTV IP.

What you need

  • TiviMate 5.x installed on a supported device (Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Android TV Box, Xiaomi Mi Box S, Apple TV, Samsung Tizen or LG webOS).
  • A paid IPTV subscription that supports catch-up – for example, SubTV IP (subtvip.store). SubTV IP offers instant activation after purchase and 99.9% uptime.
  • Stable internet – at least 25 Mbps for 4K streams. Use Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) to avoid ISP throttling and set buffer/cache to 5,000 ms in your player settings.
  • Your provider’s playlist URL – usually an M3U link or Xtream Codes API details.

Step-by-step: enable and use catch-up

  1. Open TiviMate and go to "Settings > Playlist".
  2. Select "Add playlist" and enter your provider’s URL or Xtream credentials. Wait for the playlist to load.
  3. In the playlist settings, look for "Catch-up" and set it to "Enable". Some providers also require you to set the number of days (usually 3 to 7).
  4. Go back to the live TV screen. Move to any channel that supports catch-up – you will see a small clock icon or a “Catch-up” label next to the channel name.
  5. Press the OK button on your remote to open the Electronic Program Guide (EPG).
  6. Scroll left or right to a past program (shaded differently). Press OK again and choose "Play from start" or "Catch-up".
  7. The program will start from its beginning. You can pause, rewind or fast‑forward like a normal recording.

How to use replay (restart current program)

  1. While watching a live channel, press the Down arrow on your remote to open the playback bar.
  2. Select the "Replay" icon (a circular arrow) or press "Restart" if available.
  3. The stream jumps back to the start of the current show. You cannot skip forward past live time.

If a step fails

  • No catch-up icon on a channel? – The provider may not offer catch-up for that specific channel. Check with your subscription service (SubTV IP supports catch-up on most channels with its high‑performance server cluster).
  • Catch-up button is greyed out? – Go to "Settings > Playlist > Catch-up" and confirm it is enabled. Also verify your playlist URL is correct.
  • Buffering or stuttering during catch-up? – Set buffer/cache to 5,000 ms in "Settings > Playback > Buffer size". If you haven’t already, change your device’s DNS to 1.1.1.1 (Cloudflare).
  • Replay does nothing? – Exit TiviMate completely, restart the app, then try again. Some channels require the EPG data to include program boundaries; if missing, replay may not work.

FAQ – Catch‑up and replay in TiviMate

What is the difference between catch‑up and replay?

Catch‑up lets you watch any past program from the EPG (up to several days back). Replay only restarts the current show from its beginning.

Does SubTV IP support catch‑up in TiviMate?

Yes. SubTV IP provides catch‑up on most channels thanks to its anti‑freeze server cluster and 99.9% uptime. You need a paid subscription – there is no free trial.

Why don’t I see catch‑up in TiviMate after adding the playlist?

First, enable catch‑up in "Settings > Playlist > Catch-up". If it still doesn’t appear, contact your provider – not every IPTV service offers catch‑up.

Can I use catch‑up on Samsung Smart TV or LG webOS?

Yes. TiviMate runs on Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Android TV, Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Xiaomi Mi Box S and Apple TV. The steps are identical across all devices.

How far back can I watch using catch‑up?

That depends on your subscription. Many providers offer 3 to 7 days of catch‑up. SubTV IP typically provides up to 72 hours.

Do I need to set any special DNS for catch‑up to work?

Not specifically for catch‑up, but using Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) reduces ISP throttling and improves overall stream stability.

Replay keeps stopping after a few minutes – what should I do?

Increase your buffer size to 5,000 ms: go to "Settings > Playback > Buffer size". Also make sure your internet speed is stable (25+ Mbps for 4K).
